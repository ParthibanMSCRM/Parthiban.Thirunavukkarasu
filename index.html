About

Developer depth. Functional context. Architect perspective.

I began as a hands-on Microsoft Dynamics CRM developer and progressed through technical and techno-functional consulting into Solution Architecture. This journey enables me to connect platform engineering decisions with business outcomes, governance and long-term supportability.

I specialise in shaping secure, scalable solutions across Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Azure integration services and Copilot capabilities. My work spans presales, discovery, solution options, architecture, delivery governance, technical leadership, production transition and operational readiness.

Architecture Principle

Configuration-first delivery, leveraging out-of-the-box capabilities wherever possible and using low-code or custom development only when justified, supported by architecture leadership with strong functional understanding, deep engineering capability, and integration expertise.

Architecture leadership

Target architecture, options, guardrails, estimates, traceability and design assurance.

Hands-on engineering

C#, plug-ins, JavaScript, Custom APIs, Power Fx, cloud flows and Azure components.

Collaboration

Workshops, stakeholder alignment, mentoring, presales and multidisciplinary delivery.
